# Code of Conduct

## We pledge

As contributors and maintainers we pledge to make participation in our project
and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
experience, gender, gender identity and expression, disability, nationality,
personal appearance, age, or sexual orientation.

## Standards

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and personal experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainer by opening a private issue or via the
GitHub account. The maintainer will respond and take appropriate action.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
